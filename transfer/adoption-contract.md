# TWGT transfer contract

Engineering intelligence is advisory evidence, not automatic authority.

## Admission sequence

1. Identify the original engineering problem.
2. Verify the target workload contains the same limiting constraint.
3. Record assumptions and environmental differences.
4. Locate primary evidence and implementation evidence.
5. Define a reproducible baseline.
6. Form a transfer hypothesis.
7. Implement the smallest isolated change.
8. Test normal, failure and rollback paths.
9. Measure the target workload.
10. Request human admission.

## Evidence boundary

A historical success is not a production guarantee. A benchmark is not transferable without its workload and hardware conditions. A model-generated explanation is not primary evidence.

## Required outcome record

Each adopted transfer records: source EPR, target repository, target commit, hypothesis, baseline, intervention, measurements, failure modes, decision authority and follow-up date.
