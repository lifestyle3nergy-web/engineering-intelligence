# Foundations

Research records that predate the EPR atlas. Each is a dated, authority-bounded document. They are sources that EPRs may cite. They are not EPRs themselves.

## Contents

- `TWGT_Engineering_Foundation_Research_Record.md` — record dated 2026-09-11. Networking and cybersecurity foundation. Authority boundary: intrusive activity is authorized only inside owned or explicitly permitted ethical-hacking labs.

## How to cite a foundation record

An EPR that draws from a foundation cites the record by filename and section number. Claims taken from a foundation retain the evidence class assigned in the foundation. A foundation is never treated as measured result on its own.
